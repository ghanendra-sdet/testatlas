# Foundations — Batch 3

**Modules added**: Software Testing Principles (3), Agile & Scrum Basics for QA (5), Shift-Left & Shift-Right Testing (10)
**Foundations progress**: 10 of 17 modules complete

## What Was Added

Three modules that together teach how a modern tester thinks and operates, rather than a single technique each:

- **Software Testing Principles** — the seven foundational principles (exhaustive testing is impossible, defects cluster, the pesticide paradox, and others) that shape how experienced testers reason about every situation covered later in this path.
- **Agile & Scrum Basics for QA** — how QA actually participates in Scrum: shaping stories and acceptance criteria in backlog refinement, not just testing after development finishes.
- **Shift-Left & Shift-Right Testing** — moving quality work earlier (toward requirements and design) and extending it later (into production monitoring), and why both matter for different reasons.

## Why It Matters

These three modules were identified as real gaps during a curriculum review, not filler. Without them, a learner could finish Foundations knowing testing *techniques* without ever learning the *mindset* behind when and why to apply them, or how testing actually fits into the Agile process most QA engineers work in day to day.

## Who Benefits

Anyone new to QA who needs the reasoning behind testing decisions, not just a checklist — and anyone already working in Agile/Scrum who's never had the "why" behind their team's process named explicitly.

## Curriculum Changes

This batch is part of the broader Foundations v1.0 curriculum finalization: 17 modules across six sections (Introduction → Software Development Context → Testing Fundamentals → Quality Mindset → Defect Management → Professional QA). Three previously-drafted modules (Testing Across the SDLC, Risk-Based Testing, Quality Attributes) were reordered to their new sequence positions as part of this same review — a safe move, since Docusaurus routes are slug-derived, not numeric, so no published link broke.

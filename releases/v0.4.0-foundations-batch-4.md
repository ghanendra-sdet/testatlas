## What Was Added

The Defect Management section, plus the start of Professional QA:

- **Defect Life Cycle** — the states a defect moves through from discovery to closure, with a deliberate focus on why "Fixed" and "Closed" are different claims, not the same milestone.
- **Severity vs. Priority** — two separate questions asked about every defect (how bad is it, how soon does it need fixing) and why teams that conflate them fix the wrong things first.
- **Test Strategy vs. Test Plan** — the stable, general document versus the release-specific one, and why a plan should always trace back to the strategy it implements.

## Why It Matters

These three build directly on each other: severity and priority are properties assigned during a defect's life cycle; a test plan's exit criteria are often defined in terms of open severity levels. A learner finishing this batch understands not just three isolated concepts, but how they connect into an actual defect-management and planning workflow.

## Who Benefits

QA engineers who need to run (or participate in) real defect triage, and anyone preparing for interview questions on defect management — one of the most commonly asked topic areas in QA interviews.

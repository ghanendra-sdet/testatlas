## What Was Added

The closing section of Foundations:

- **QA Metrics & Measurement** — which QA metrics actually correlate with quality versus which ones are easy to game, including a direct application of Goodhart's Law to testing.
- **Common QA Terminology** — a consolidated, categorized glossary of every term introduced across the path, each linked back to its source module. Deliberately breaks from Why-What-When-How, since it introduces nothing new.
- **QA Career Roadmap** — how QA careers branch into SDET, Senior QA (IC), and QA Lead (management) tracks, and how to recognize genuine signal for which one fits.
- **Testing Myths & Misconceptions** — the capstone. Seven widely-believed half-truths, each debunked using a concept taught earlier in the path, with a real-world story and an interview angle.

## Why It Matters

This batch closes the loop the whole path opened. Metrics answers "how is QA work evaluated." Terminology consolidates sixteen modules of vocabulary into one reference. Career Roadmap answers the question the role archetypes in Module 2 raised but couldn't yet answer. And Myths deliberately teaches nothing new — it's designed to prove, to the learner and to themselves, that the first sixteen modules actually became instinct.

## Who Benefits

Anyone finishing Foundations end to end — this batch is what turns sixteen modules of concepts into a felt sense of "I actually understand this now," which is the whole point of a capstone.

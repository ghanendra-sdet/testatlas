# Section 5 Review

Section 5 shifted from what to say to how it's delivered — pacing and composure, evaluating and negotiating an offer, and the recurring patterns that connect every earlier section's own individual lessons.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these five scenarios before checking the [Section 5 Solutions](/learning-paths/interview-preparation/section-5-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The Filled Silence**

A candidate, given a genuinely hard question, fills the silence with filler words instead of pausing. What should they have done instead, and why does it matter even if the eventual answer is correct?

**Scenario 2: The Headline Number**

A candidate accepts an offer based on the base salary alone, without reading the full compensation structure. What did they likely miss, and how should they have approached it?

**Scenario 3: One Pattern, Many Rounds**

A candidate works silently through a live SQL exercise, then later gives an unstructured behavioral answer. What single underlying pattern connects both mistakes?

**Scenario 4: The Vague Negotiation**

A candidate asks for a higher salary by saying "I feel like I deserve more." What's missing from this request?

**Scenario 5: Uneven Preparation**

A candidate receives feedback that their technical answers were strong but their behavioral and scenario answers were noticeably weaker. What does this module suggest was the likely cause?

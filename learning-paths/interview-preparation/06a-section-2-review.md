# Section 2 Review

Section 2 applied Manual Testing's own technique — test design, defect handling, and process reasoning — directly to live interview conditions.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these five scenarios before checking the [Section 2 Solutions](/learning-paths/interview-preparation/section-2-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The List Without a Method**

A candidate, asked to design test cases for a quantity field accepting 1–99 units, lists "test 1, test 99, test 0, test 100, test a negative number." What's missing from this answer, even though the values themselves are mostly correct?

**Scenario 2: Impressive But Unconvincing**

A candidate defines severity and priority correctly but hesitates badly when asked for an example where they'd diverge. What should they have prepared in advance?

**Scenario 3: The Blame Frame**

A candidate describes a developer disagreement by saying "they just refused to acknowledge the bug for weeks." What's wrong with this framing, regardless of who was actually right?

**Scenario 4: Diagram, No Reasoning**

A candidate answers "what does shift-left mean" by defining the term accurately but never gives an example. What's missing?

**Scenario 5: Working in Silence**

A candidate, given a live test-design exercise, works quietly for two minutes and then presents a finished list. What specific interview expectation does this violate?

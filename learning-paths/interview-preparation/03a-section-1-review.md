# Section 1 Review

Section 1 set the foundation for everything in this path: recognizing what kind of round you're actually in, presenting your own work credibly under probing, and structuring behavioral stories so the useful information is actually findable.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these five scenarios before checking the [Section 1 Solutions](/learning-paths/interview-preparation/section-1-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The Underspecified Prompt**

An interviewer asks, with no further detail, "How would you test our new checkout redesign?" What round type is this, and what should you do before answering the substance of the question?

**Scenario 2: The Number That Doesn't Hold Up**

A candidate's resume says "reduced regression testing time by 40%." Asked to explain how, they can only restate the number. What's missing, and how should they have prepared for this?

**Scenario 3: The Unstructured Story**

A candidate answers a behavioral question with a long, chronological account that never clearly states what they specifically did. What's the fix, and which part of their answer needed the most attention?

**Scenario 4: Too Much Background**

A candidate spends over a minute describing the project and team context before getting to what they actually did. What STAR guidance does this violate?

**Scenario 5: A Real Story, Poorly Told**

A candidate has a genuinely strong example of catching a critical defect, but the interviewer's feedback says the story was "hard to follow." What is most likely to have gone wrong, given this section's own lessons?

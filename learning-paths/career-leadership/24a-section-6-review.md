# Section 6 Review

Section 6 built the core team-management skills every QA leadership track eventually needs: hiring for real judgment, making performance reviews genuinely reflect ongoing attention, giving timely and specific feedback, and coaching people toward their own defined goals.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these four scenarios before checking the [Section 6 Solutions](/learning-paths/career-leadership/section-6-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The Checklist Rejection**

A hiring manager evaluates every QA candidate against a checklist of 15 specific tools, and a strong candidate with different tool experience but excellent reasoning is rejected. What should the hiring process have assessed instead?

**Scenario 2: The Reconstructed Review**

A QA Manager writes performance reviews once a year, based on vague, general impressions reconstructed from memory shortly before each review is due. What's missing, and what should change?

**Scenario 3: The Six-Month Delay**

A QA Lead notices a recurring, specific issue in a team member's bug reports in March but waits until the September review to mention it. What's the cost of this delay?

**Scenario 4: The Forgotten Goal**

A team member mentioned a career goal in passing eight months ago, and it hasn't come up in any one-on-one since. What practice would have prevented this?

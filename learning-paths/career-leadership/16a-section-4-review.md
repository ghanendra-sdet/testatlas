# Section 4 Review

Section 4 extended engineering-excellence practices from individual technique to organizational scale: driving shift-left structurally, extending quality beyond release, building genuine Quality Engineering ownership, and governing standards without heavyweight bureaucracy.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these four scenarios before checking the [Section 4 Solutions](/learning-paths/career-leadership/section-4-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The Company-Wide Email**

A VP asks a QA Manager to "make the organization shift-left" after reading an industry article. The QA Manager sends a company-wide email asking teams to write more tests earlier. What's missing from this approach?

**Scenario 2: The Production Surprise**

A QA Lead's team ships a feature that passes all pre-release testing, but a performance issue only appears under real production load two weeks later. What practice would have caught this sooner, and how?

**Scenario 3: The Rebrand That Didn't Change Anything**

A company rebrands its QA team as "Quality Engineering" and updates job titles, but developers still treat all testing as that team's sole responsibility a year later. What was missing from the rebrand?

**Scenario 4: The Governance Slowdown**

A Head of QA introduces a mandatory, detailed governance review for every test plan across a 100-engineer organization, and release velocity drops sharply within a quarter. What went wrong?

# Section 9 Review

Section 9 covered modern leadership challenges: leading AI-assisted testing adoption and automation transformation honestly, and building the engineering culture that determines whether quality practices actually take hold.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these two scenarios before checking the [Section 9 Solutions](/learning-paths/career-leadership/section-9-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The Overstated Announcement**

A QA Manager, under pressure to demonstrate innovation, announces that AI-assisted test generation will "replace manual test design" within the quarter, without piloting the tools first. What should they have done instead?

**Scenario 2: The Reluctant Team**

A QA Manager notices their team has become reluctant to flag ambiguous or borderline defects, tending to quietly work around them instead. Investigation traces this to an incident over a year earlier where an engineer was visibly, if unintentionally, singled out for surfacing a defect. What's the fix?

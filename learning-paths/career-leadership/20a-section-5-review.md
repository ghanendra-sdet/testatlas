# Section 5 Review

Section 5 built a complete metrics and reporting discipline: choosing quality KPIs and defect metrics that reflect real outcomes, measuring automation health and release readiness beyond raw counts, and translating that data correctly for both executive and engineering audiences.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these four scenarios before checking the [Section 5 Solutions](/learning-paths/career-leadership/section-5-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The Growing Test-Case Count**

A QA Manager's quality report leads with "test cases executed this quarter: 3,200, up from 2,100 last year," presented as a success story. What's missing from this metric, and what should replace it?

**Scenario 2: The Ignored Red Suite**

An automated suite has grown to 2,000 tests, but developers have started ignoring red results, assuming they're probably flaky. What metric would diagnose this, and what should the response be?

**Scenario 3: The Deck Nobody Discusses**

A fifteen-slide, highly detailed quality deck presented quarterly to executive leadership consistently generates no discussion or follow-up decisions. What's the likely cause?

**Scenario 4: The Ignored Engineering Report**

An engineering team ignores a weekly quality report because it's just a single flake-rate percentage with no further detail. What's missing, and how should it be fixed?

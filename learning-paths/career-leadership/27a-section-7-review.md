# Section 7 Review

Section 7 built the cross-functional relationship skills every QA leadership track eventually needs: working effectively with product and engineering, coordinating shared infrastructure with DevOps, managing stakeholders broadly, and communicating with executives under real time pressure.

## Knowledge Check

Work through these three scenarios before checking the [Section 7 Solutions](/learning-paths/career-leadership/section-7-solutions).

**Scenario 1: The Excluded QA Team**

A QA Lead's team has grown adversarial with a product team, since QA seems to only ever raise problems and is now being excluded from early planning conversations. What should the QA Lead do differently?

**Scenario 2: The Unowned Pipeline**

QA and DevOps have each informally assumed the other is responsible for a persistently unreliable CI/CD pipeline, and the problem has gone unresolved for months. What's the fix?

**Scenario 3: The Over-Explained Answer**

A QA Manager, asked directly in an executive meeting whether a release is ready, responds with a three-minute explanation of test coverage before actually answering the question. What should they have done instead?

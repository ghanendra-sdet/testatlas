# Code of Conduct

TestAtlas is built by people learning and practicing software testing together. This document states, plainly, what that requires from everyone involved — contributors, reviewers, maintainers, and anyone participating in an issue, pull request, or discussion.

This is the enforcement-facing companion to [PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md](PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md)'s "Openness with respect" value and [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)'s conflict-resolution process — read those for the project's broader standards; this document exists so expected behavior and how to report a problem are both easy to find on their own.

## Our Standard

Participation in TestAtlas means:

- **Critique ideas, not people.** Strong technical disagreement is welcome and expected — a review that says "this example doesn't reflect how the feature actually fails" is doing its job. A review that attacks the person who wrote it is not.
- **Assume good faith, especially with newer contributors.** A question that seems basic to you may be the first time someone's asked it. Answer it the way you'd have wanted it answered.
- **Give feedback that's specific and actionable.** "This is wrong" without saying what's wrong or why helps no one. See [REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md](REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md) for the standard TestAtlas reviews are held to.
- **Respect scope and other people's time.** Keep changes focused, respond to review feedback in good faith, and don't reopen a settled decision without new evidence.

The following are not acceptable, in any TestAtlas space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, or any other project-affiliated communication):

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberately bad-faith behavior
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussion, review, or maintainer work
- Any conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome participating

This list isn't exhaustive — if something isn't explicitly named here but clearly violates the spirit of "openness with respect," it's still a violation.

## Scope

This applies to every TestAtlas space: the repository itself (issues, pull requests, code review, commit messages), any associated discussion forum, and any other venue where someone is participating as a TestAtlas contributor or representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the project maintainers as described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Reports are handled with as much privacy as the situation allows. You do not need to have a perfect account of events to report something — describe what happened as best you can.

## Enforcement

Reports are reviewed by project maintainers, following [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)'s conflict-resolution and maintainer-decision process. Responses are proportionate to the behavior and may include a private conversation, a public correction, temporary limits on participation, or removal from the project's spaces — maintainers use judgment based on severity and pattern, not a fixed script.

Maintainers who violate this Code of Conduct are held to the same standard as any other participant, per [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)'s maintainer removal process.
